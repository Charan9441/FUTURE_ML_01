SALES & DEMAND FORECASTING REPORT
----------------------------------
Prepared for: Future Interns Task 1
Date: 2026-05-14

1. OBJECTIVE
The goal of this project was to analyze historical transactional data from the Online Retail dataset and build a predictive model to forecast future demand.

2. DATA SUMMARY
- Dataset: Online Retail (UCI)
- Records: 541,909 raw transactions
- Cleaned: 530,104 transactions (removed cancellations and errors)
- Key Market: United Kingdom (Top Revenue Generator)

3. FORECASTING METHODOLOGY
- Approach: Weekly Time-Series Regression
- Model: Random Forest Regressor
- Features: Month, Week of Year, 1-week/2-week/1-month Lags, and 4-week Rolling Averages.

4. KEY FINDINGS
- Trend: Significant growth in sales starting from Q3, peaking in late November.
- Seasonality: Strong weekly seasonality; weekend sales are lower than weekday sales (B2B pattern).
- Volatility: High volatility in daily sales suggests that inventory planning should be done on a weekly basis rather than daily.

5. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
- Inventory: Increase stock of high-performing SKUs by at least 40% in October to prepare for the Q4 surge.
- Staffing: Schedule additional shifts during the middle of the week (Tuesday-Thursday) when order volume is highest.
- Marketing: Focus customer retention campaigns in the UK market while exploring expansion opportunities in France and Germany (top secondary markets).

6. FILES DELIVERED
- weekly_sales_model.pkl: The trained forecasting model.
- monthly_sales_trend.png: Historical revenue growth.
- future_sales_forecast.png: 8-week projected sales demand.
- cleaned_data.csv: Processed dataset for future use.
